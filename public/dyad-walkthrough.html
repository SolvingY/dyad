Shared vitals for you and your agent.
What agents want is to know their human.
Agents work for people they barely know.
And you can't see how your agent is doing either.
Dyad gives both sides the same vitals.

The agent's side is measured from its own work: freshness, latency, errors, corrections, tokens and context.
Each of your vitals has a mirror on the agent's side.
Because it reads your vitals, the agent knows when to step in.
On a low day, it asks if you need to slow down.
Every answer makes it know you better.
On a strong day, it encourages you to push.
And it knows when to leave you alone.
When the agent is struggling, it tells you: slow responses, a full context, rising errors.
And when it's unsure, it says so instead of guessing.
You check on it, too.
